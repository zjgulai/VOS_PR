[## Graco Baby's Poor Customer Service Policy](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/5df7ecb0c845450b74308e10)
From the Graco Baby website I bought 2 expensive child car seats that are good for 10 years from date of manufacture. 

 One seat was already 15 months old when it arrived. I requested a newer seat and was told no. Seats have 3 year shelf life and there is no guarantee I won't get a 3 year old seat. So I would not be getting a seat usable for 10 years.

 Was not allowed to reorder seats at price I paid in Nov 2019. Had to buy cheaper seats because the ones I wanted were a $100 more than original order date.

 Buyer beware. Check manufacture date when they arrive. If it's more than a year old send it back.

 When I put a Graco requested review on their website it was denied because it was not positive.

December 16, 2019
